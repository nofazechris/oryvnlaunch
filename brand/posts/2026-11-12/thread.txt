Three presets, same raise, different steepness. Price multiple to graduation: 4x, 9x, 25x.

================ TWEET 3 · attach 03-bug.png ================
What bit us: we asked a real Starknet node for one contract's events and got every event on the chain. 45 instead of 3.

Our filter was in the wrong place and the node never complained. A fake node would have hidden it.

Also caught: a graduated token showing 25% progress.
